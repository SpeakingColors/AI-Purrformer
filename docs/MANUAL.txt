AI PURRFORMER: QUICK MANUAL
===========================

TWO PLUGINS
AI Purrformer        (effect)      your recorded voice becomes the AI voice. Start here.
AI Purrformer Sing   (instrument)  MIDI notes and lyrics in, AI voice out. Works in every DAW.
Everything runs on your computer. Start the Engine first (Start menu: AI Purrformer Engine).

AI PURRFORMER (the effect): 5 STEPS
1. Put it as an effect on the track where you sing. The header says "Engine: running".
2. Optional: send the master or a track to the plugin's SIDECHAIN. It finds the key and the
   chords. Optional: send MIDI from another track, the voice then follows those notes.
3. Press RECORD, press play in your DAW and sing, press STOP. A loop jump also ends it.
4. Pick a voice (up to three). "Snap to key": 0% = as you sang, 100% = every note on the key.
   "Follow MIDI": how closely to follow MIDI notes if you sent any.
5. Press RENDER. When done, DRAG the big pink box onto any track in your DAW. A .wav is also
   saved on your computer, and "Save .wav..." saves a copy where you want.
The result starts where your recording started: drop it at the same bar. You can also press
play and hear it from the plugin, in time with the DAW. It is not live: it records, then renders.
Pictures: see docs/USE-IN-YOUR-DAW.md in the repository.

AI PURRFORMER SING (the instrument): THE IDEA
You give it a melody as MIDI (and optional lyrics). It sings that melody with an AI voice that is
in tune and on time. Your own voice is never used for pitch. Everything runs on your computer.
The plugin (what you see in your DAW) talks to the Engine (the black window) on your computer.

FIRST SONG (5 steps)
1. Make an instrument track with AI Purrformer Sing. Put a MIDI clip with ONE melody line on it
   (one note at a time).
2. In the plugin, press "Capture from host", press play in your DAW, let the clip play once,
   then press "Stop capturing". You can also load a .mid file with "Open .mid...".
3. Type words on the Lyrics tab (optional). The plugin counts syllables and tells you if the
   words fit the notes.
4. Press "Render" and wait for the status line to say it is done.
5. Press play in your DAW. The singer follows the DAW position, so it stays in time.
   If you change the tempo, capture and render again.

THE FOUR TABS
SING
  Mode: "Melody leads" = your MIDI decides the notes (default). "Voice leads" = the voice decides.
  Voices 1, 2, 3 with faders: mix up to three voices. Weights are normalised.
  Blend: "Layer (choir)" sings with each voice and mixes them (works with any voice).
         "One new voice (test)" blends the voices into one new voice (experimental).
  Piano roll and waveform show your notes and the rendered audio.
VOICE FX
  Robot (autotune effect): Retune speed (left = natural glide, right = instant hard robot),
    Dry/wet (left = clean voice, right = all robot).
  Supersaw: Copies (3 to 9 stacked voices), Detune (up to 50 cents on the outer copies),
    Spread (pan across left/right), Widen (extra side level), Stack mix.
  Dirt: Drive (warm to heavy), Bitcrush, Artifacts (stutters, dropouts, buzz), Dirt mix.
  Each effect has an On/Off switch. The little pictures move with the knobs.
LYRICS
  Notepad for your words with a syllable count against your notes.
VOICES
  List of voices. Click one, then "Use as voice 1/2/3".
  "Import .wav...": add a clean clip of 1 to 60 seconds. You must tick that you own the voice or
  have permission or a licence. Do not use other people's voices without permission.
  "Delete selected" removes your own voices (not built-in ones).

VOICES THAT COME WITH IT
- Built-in test voices (synthetic tones): for checking that everything works.
- Starter singers from VocalSet (CC BY 4.0): sustained vowel clips, good for voice colour, not for
  clear words. Credit: VocalSet, Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl, Bryan Pardo (ISMIR 2018).

TIPS
- One note at a time sounds best. With the real voice, overlapping notes are cut short so the singer
  never sings two notes at once, and notes shorter than about 0.04 s are dropped.
- Everything is local: no accounts, no cloud, no internet.

KNOWN LIMITS (version 0.1)
- Real AI singing has not been tested on a graphics card yet. Until then the test voices work
  and the real voice engine is experimental.
- English only for now.
- Changing the DAW tempo after rendering needs a new render.
- Tested hosts: not yet. Please report what works for you.
